The isolated laboratory follows the service topology documented in [PeerDB](https://github.com/PeerDB-io/peerdb/tree/42e190b897bf073ba49f1460f83f669c97a4dbad), licensed under Elastic License 2.0. Rowglass does not copy or modify PeerDB application code. PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, Temporal and the MinIO-compatible lab service are fetched separately using Compose; review their licenses for any deployment or redistribution.

Rust dependencies and exact resolved versions are recorded in `Cargo.lock`; Python depends on pinned psycopg2-binary 2.9.11. The transport uses the public libpq C interface loaded from that driver extension. Documentation links are references, not endorsements. All included row datasets are generated by the Rowglass lab or benchmark scripts. No third-party customer datasets are included.
